# Ayurveda Formulations, Traditional Knowledge and IP

Classical Ayurvedic knowledge and formulations may be traditional knowledge and can be relevant prior art for patent examination. Before planning a patent filing, identify the exact formulation, ingredients, process and stated technical contribution, and check relevant classical, TKDL, patent and non-patent literature with appropriate professional access.

IP protection is not limited to patents. A distinctive brand may be considered for trademark protection; original manuals, artwork and content may be relevant to copyright; and confidential manufacturing know-how may need trade-secret controls such as restricted access and written confidentiality arrangements. The suitable route depends on facts and legal review.

An invention relating to an Ayurvedic formulation must still meet patentability requirements and avoid statutory exclusions. A traditional ingredient list or a known aggregation does not automatically establish novelty or inventive step. Patent claims and evidence should be reviewed against the Patents Act and current IP India examination practice.

AYUSH compliance and IP are separate assessments. This knowledge base does not provide diagnosis, treatment, dosage, therapeutic claims, manufacturing permission, or product approval advice. Consult the relevant AYUSH authority and qualified professionals for current regulatory requirements.
