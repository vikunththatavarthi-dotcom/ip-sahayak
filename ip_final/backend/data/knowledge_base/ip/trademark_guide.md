# Trademark Registration in India

## What is a Trademark?
A trademark is any sign capable of distinguishing the goods or services of one enterprise from those of other enterprises. It can be a word, name, symbol, device, or any combination thereof.

## Types of Trademarks
1. **Word Mark**: Text-based marks (brand names)
2. **Device Mark**: Symbols, logos, or designs
3. **Combination Mark**: Words combined with symbols
4. **Collective Mark**: Used by a group/association
5. **Certification Mark**: Certifies quality/characteristics of goods

## Registration Process
1. File trademark application with Trademark Office
2. Examination for absolute grounds (distinctiveness, non-offensive)
3. Advertisement in Trademark Journal
4. Opposition period (4 months)
5. Grant of registration (valid for 10 years, renewable)

## NICE Classification
All goods and services are classified into 45 classes under the Nice Classification System. Applicants must specify which classes their trademark covers.

## Key Exclusions (Section 9)
- Generic/descriptive marks
- Marks depicting country flags
- Marks without distinctive character
- Marks identical to well-known marks

## Authority
Indian Trademark Office / IP India
