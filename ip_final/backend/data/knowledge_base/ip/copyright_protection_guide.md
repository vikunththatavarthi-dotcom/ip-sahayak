# Copyright Protection and Software Licensing in India

Copyright in India is governed by the Copyright Act, 1957 (as amended). Copyright protects original literary, dramatic, musical, artistic works, cinematograph films, and sound recordings from unauthorized reproduction, adaptation, publication, or distribution.

## Scope of Protection for Technology & Business Works
1. **Software & Computer Code**: Under Section 2(o), computer software programs, source code, object code, databases, and system documentation are recognized and protected as "literary works".
2. **Product Manuals & Documentation**: Product instruction sheets, user manuals, brochures, website text copy, and scientific write-ups receive literary copyright protection.
3. **Labels & Visual Artwork**: Logos, package graphics, 2D artistic illustrations, and diagrams are protected as "artistic works" under Section 2(c).

## Ownership, Authorship & Work-for-Hire
- **Author vs Owner (Section 17)**: The author is the first owner of copyright, UNLESS the work is created by an employee in the course of employment under a contract of service, in which case the employer is the first owner.
- **Independent Contractors**: For third-party software developers or design agencies, a formal written Copyright Assignment Agreement is legally mandatory to transfer ownership to the client.

## Term of Protection
- For literary, dramatic, musical, and artistic works: Lifetime of the author plus **60 years** after the author's death.
- For published anonymous works, cinematograph films, and government works: 60 years from the year of publication.

## Fair Dealing Exemptions (Section 52)
Section 52 provides statutory exemptions where use is not an infringement:
- Private or personal use, including research.
- Criticism, review, or reporting of current events.
- Reverse engineering or making backup copies of computer programs for lawful interoperability.
